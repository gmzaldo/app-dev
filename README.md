# My Favorite Anime Movie: Your Name

## About the Movie

**Your Name (Kimi no Na wa)** is a Japanese animated romantic fantasy movie released in 2016. It was directed by Makoto Shinkai.

![Your Name Movie Poster](https://posteritati.com/film/17800/your-name?srsltid=AYCX9vIfr8oEKVpisbl-2yIi2ekfFdmoEoaUxPxjjGFzQvi90QCw5V0r)

## Movie Information

- **Title:** Your Name
- **Japanese Title:** Kimi no Na wa
- **Director:** Makoto Shinkai
- **Release Year:** 2016
- **Genre:** Romance, Fantasy, Drama, Animation

## Main Characters

1. Taki Tachibana
2. Mitsuha Miyamizu

## Movie Description

Your Name is about two teenagers, Taki and Mitsuha, who mysteriously start switching bodies even though they live in different places. As they try to understand what is happening, they develop a special connection and search for each other.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like Your Name because of its beautiful animation, interesting story, and emotional scenes. It also shows how important memories and connections with other people are. The music and the ending make the movie more memorable for me.
